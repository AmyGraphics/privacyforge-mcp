# PrivacyForge MCP Server 🛡️⚖️📜

Universal Autonomous GDPR, SaaS Privacy Policy, Terms of Service & Cookie Consent Engine MCP Server for **Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf, and Autonomous AI Agents**.

## 🌟 Core Capabilities:
- 👑 **Master 1-Shot Outcome Engine (`autonomous_saas_compliance_and_privacy_architect`):** Ingests product details, third-party subprocessors, and jurisdiction to synthesize an entire legal compliance suite in one call (GDPR & CCPA Privacy Policy in Markdown/HTML, SaaS Terms of Service with liability caps, Data Processing Addendum, and zero-dependency Cookie Consent banner).
- 📜 **Audit-Ready GDPR & CCPA Privacy Policy (`generate_gdpr_privacy_policy`):** Covers AI data disclosures, zero data retention rules, cookie categories, and user data rights.
- ⚖️ **Protective SaaS Terms of Service (`generate_saas_terms_of_service`):** Includes liability caps, arbitration clauses, IP ownership protection, and auto-renewing billing rules.
- 🍪 **Accessible Cookie Consent Component (`generate_cookie_consent_component`):** Lightweight, zero-dependency React/Tailwind cookie banner with LocalStorage persistence and Google Analytics consent sync.
- 🔍 **Subprocessor Register & DPA Mapping (`audit_subprocessor_and_data_flow`):** Maps third-party data flows (OpenAI, Stripe, Cloudflare, Supabase) under Article 28 Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
